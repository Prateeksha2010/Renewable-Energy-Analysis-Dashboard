🌱 Renewable Energy Analysis Dashboard
📊 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive Renewable Energy Analysis Dashboard built to evaluate the environmental, economic, and employment impact of different renewable energy sources.

The dashboard provides insights into sustainability metrics, pollution reduction, investment trends, and job creation across multiple renewable energy types such as Solar, Wind, Hydro, Biomass, Geothermal, Tidal, and Wave energy.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze the environmental impact of renewable energy

Evaluate economic investments and returns

Measure job creation and employment efficiency

Compare performance across different energy sources

Enable data-driven sustainability decisions
